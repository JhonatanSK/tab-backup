## Long description

Your tabs, automatically backed up.

TabBackup saves local snapshots of your open Chrome tabs, windows and groups, so you can return to a saved session after an accidental close or browser restart.

• Automatic local snapshots when your tabs change, plus a periodic safety check.
• Keep your latest 20 distinct snapshots and create a backup whenever you need one.
• Restore tabs into new windows, including order, pinned tabs and supported tab groups.
• Review saved tabs and filter your backup library by title or URL.
• Export a .tabbackup.json file for a separate copy, or preview and import one later.
• No account, cloud upload or remote analytics. Your tab data stays on this device.

Restore keeps your current session open and reports skipped URLs, failed tabs and verification warnings. Internal Chrome pages, extension URLs and unsupported pages are skipped. Website loading, sign-ins, unsaved forms, page scripts, scroll position and video state are not restored.

Local snapshots are internal extension storage. Uninstalling TabBackup removes them. Export important backups and keep the files private: URLs and titles may contain sensitive information. Recent changes may not have been saved before a crash. Recovery is not guaranteed.

TabBackup uses access to tab metadata and tab groups only to provide backup and restore. Incognito windows are excluded. It does not read page content, cookies or site storage.
